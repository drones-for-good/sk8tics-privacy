Delete your Sk8tics account

Last updated 4 September 2026

You can have your Sk8tics account and everything in it deleted at any time. You do not have to give a reason.

How to ask

Email us from the address your account uses, so we know the request is really from you.

Email privacy@dronesforgood.global

Include the username on the account. That is all we need.

If the account belongs to a skater under 18, a parent or guardian can make this request instead. Say which skater the request is for, and we will confirm with the account before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to the account, permanently:

  • Sign-in details — email address and password
  • Profile — username and name
  • Seasons, programs, and the elements in them
  • Every logged practice session and every mark in it
  • Links to any coaches
  • Scores and notes a coach recorded about the skater

What stays, briefly

Deleted records can survive in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before those backups are overwritten. Nobody reads them in that window; they are only there so we could recover from a failure. After 30 days no copy remains.

How long it takes

We delete accounts within 7 days of a request, and reply to confirm when it is done. If we need to check something with you first, we will say so within those 7 days.

Before you go

Deletion cannot be undone, and a season of training records cannot be rebuilt. If you only want to stop a coach seeing your log, you do not need to delete your account — removing the coach link in the app ends their access immediately and keeps your history.
